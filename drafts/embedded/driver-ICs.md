A driver integrated circuit (IC) is a special IC that "drives" other circuits,
by different methods like changing current, changing voltage or varying width
of the electrical pulses in a signal (PWM).

Examples of driver ICs:
  - LED drivers:
    - Might use current to control LEDs
    - Might use PWM to control LEDs
  - RS-232 ICs:
    - Use voltage control RS-232 connections.
  - Relay drivers:
    - Use current and voltage to energize the coil of the relay.
